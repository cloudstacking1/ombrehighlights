What Are Ombre Highlights?

Ombre highlights are a trendy and versatile hair color technique where your hair gradually transitions from a darker shade at the roots to a lighter color at the ends. This gradient style offers a natural-looking fade that mimics sun exposure, making it a favorite for those who want a low-maintenance, high-impact look.

Whether you’re aiming for dramatic contrast or a subtle transition, our stylists at Highlights Hair NYC will help you find the right shade and placement to elevate your overall appearance.

Ombre Highlights vs. Balayage Highlights

While both ombre highlights and balayage highlights add dimension and depth to your hair, they differ in technique:

  • Ombre is a gradual fade from dark to light.
  • Balayage is a hand-painted method that creates a sun-kissed, natural look with no harsh lines.

At Highlights Hair NYC, we offer both techniques, customizing each treatment based on your hair length, texture, and goals.
